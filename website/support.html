Support

We usually reply within a day.

Email support@gimmegolf.app with what happened and, if you can, a screenshot. Beta testers can also send feedback straight from TestFlight by taking a screenshot in Gimme.

Signing in

Enter your email and Gimme sends you a 6-digit code. If it hasn't arrived after a minute, check spam, then request a new one.

Distances look off

Make sure Location is set to "While Using" or "Always" with Precise Location on, in Settings › Gimme › Location.

A prize drop won't unlock

Drops unlock only during a round you've started in Gimme, when you're within the drop area around the green. Some drops also need par or birdie on that hole, so enter your score first.

Reporting a post or a person

Tap ⋯ on any post or comment and choose Report, or Block to hide that person. We review every report within 24 hours.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile › Delete account. This removes your account and everything you've posted. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Gimme

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

Gimme is a golf app for GPS distances, scoring with friends, a community feed, live rounds, and prizes from golf brands. This policy explains what Gimme collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Email address. Used to send you a sign-in code and to identify your account. We don't email you marketing.
  • Profile. Your name, handle, avatar, handicap and home course. Other golfers can see your profile so they can add you as a friend.
  • Payment handles. Your Venmo and Cash App usernames, if you add them, so friends in your round can pay you for side games. Gimme never sees or holds money.
  • Precise location, only during a round. Used to show distances to the green, to check that you're really on a hole when you unlock a prize drop, and, only while you have "Let friends watch" turned on, to show your friends where you are on the hole. Your position is removed from our servers when you stop going live.
  • Rounds and activity. Scores, round chat, feed posts, comments, reactions, and which friends you play with.
  • Video clips. Clips you choose to post. A clip posted to "Everyone" can be watched by anyone who has its link; other posts are shown only to you and your friends.
  • Live camera and microphone. Only while you choose to stream your round. The stream goes to friends watching through LiveKit and is not recorded by Gimme.
  • Prize claims. Which drops you unlock and claim, and your claim codes.
  • Reports and blocks. Content you report and people you block, so we can review reports and hide blocked people from you.

What we don't do

  • We don't track you across other apps or websites, and there are no ad networks in Gimme.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't collect location outside of a round you start.

Who sees your information

  • Your friends see your posts, rounds, and, while you're live, your position, scores and stream.
  • Brands that sponsor a drop see how many golfers reached and claimed it, and the claim code you bring to them to redeem the prize. They don't get your location history or your contacts. If you claim a prize on a brand's website, that brand's own privacy policy applies there.
  • Service providers that run Gimme for us: Supabase (accounts, database and file storage), LiveKit (live video), and Apple (app delivery, TestFlight and maps). They process data only to provide their service to us.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile › Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, posts, clips, comments, reactions, chat messages, scores and prize claims. Rounds you started with friends stay with the friends who played them, without your scores. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Keeping data

We keep your information while your account exists. Live positions are deleted when you stop going live. Reports are kept as long as needed to keep the community safe.

Children

Gimme is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app when the change is significant.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@gimmegolf.app
